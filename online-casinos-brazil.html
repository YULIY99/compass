Status: Regulated but contested. Brazil launched a federal licensing regime under Law 14.790/2023 (SPA/Ministry of Finance) in January 2025. As of April 2026, licensed operators are barred from accepting crypto, credit cards and BNPL — only PIX, TED and debit/prepaid cards are allowed. TL;DR: Brazil regulates online betting and casino gaming federally via the SPA, but the rules are actively changing — crypto and credit cards are now banned for licensed operators, and casino-game legality itself is under legislative review.

How It Works

Brazil's Secretaria de Prêmios e Apostas (SPA), under the Ministry of Finance, issues licenses (SPA license) for fixed-odds betting and online gaming, taxed at 12% of gross gaming revenue. The regime went live January 2025, but has faced fast-moving amendments, including a payment-method restriction in April 2026 and an active legislative push to ban online casino games specifically.

Payment Methods

PIX is the dominant payment rail — instant, 24/7 bank transfers. As of April 2026, licensed operators cannot legally accept crypto, credit cards, or buy-now-pay-later; only PIX, TED and debit/prepaid cards are compliant.

FAQ

Is online casino gambling still legal in Brazil?

As of writing, yes for SPA-licensed operators, but a bill in Congress could restrict or ban online casino games while preserving sports betting — status can change quickly.

Can I use crypto to deposit at a Brazilian casino?

No. Since April 2026, SPA-licensed operators cannot accept crypto, credit cards, or BNPL. Use PIX, TED, or debit/prepaid cards.

What is PIX?

PIX is Brazil's instant payment system, run by the Central Bank, enabling 24/7 transfers used for most casino deposits and withdrawals.
