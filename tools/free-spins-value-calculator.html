How It Works

Free spins pay out according to the game's RTP, so 50 spins at $0.20 on a 96% RTP slot are expected to win about $9.60. Then the casino applies wagering to those winnings: 35x on $9.60 means $336 of play-through, which costs about $13.44 in expected losses at 96% RTP. Subtract that from the winnings and you get the real cash value. No wagering (x0)? Then the spins are worth their full expected winnings.
