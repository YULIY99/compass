How It Works

Mexico's gaming law dates to 1947 and was not written with online casinos in mind. SEGOB (Secretaría de Gobernación) issues permits mainly for land-based and remote betting operations tied to a physical permit holder. In practice, the majority of online casino traffic in Mexico goes to offshore-licensed sites, which operate in a legal grey zone rather than being explicitly illegal for players.

Payment Methods

SPEI (via CLABE bank accounts) is the standard local bank-transfer rail; OXXO cash payments and crypto are also common for offshore operators.

FAQ

Is it legal for me to play at an online casino in Mexico?

Playing at an offshore-licensed casino is not explicitly criminalized for individual players, though the operator itself may lack a Mexican permit.

What is SPEI?

SPEI is Mexico's interbank electronic payment system, used for instant bank transfers to and from casino accounts via a CLABE number.

Can I pay with cash in Mexico?

Yes, many offshore operators support OXXO cash deposit vouchers alongside SPEI and crypto.
