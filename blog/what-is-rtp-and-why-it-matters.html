What Is RTP and Why It Matters

RTP (Return to Player) is one of the most useful numbers for judging a slot's long-term payout behavior.

What does RTP mean?

RTP is the theoretical percentage of all wagered money a slot returns to players over millions of spins. A 96% RTP means, on average, $96 is paid back for every $100 wagered — over the long run, not in a single session.

Does RTP predict a single session?

No. RTP is a long-run statistical average. Volatility (how big and how often wins occur) matters more for short-session experience than RTP alone.
