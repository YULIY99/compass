Understanding Wagering Requirements

Wagering requirements determine how much you must bet before withdrawing bonus winnings — and they're the single biggest factor in whether a bonus is actually worth claiming.

How does a wagering multiplier work?

If you get a $100 bonus with 35x wagering, you must wager $3,500 in total before withdrawing any winnings from that bonus.

Do all games count equally?

No. Slots usually count 100%, while table games and live dealer games often count 10-20% or are excluded entirely — always check terms.
