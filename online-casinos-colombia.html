Status: Fully regulated. Colombia was one of the first Latin American countries to legalize and regulate online gambling, overseen by Coljuegos.
TL;DR: Online gambling is fully legal and regulated in Colombia. Operators need a Coljuegos license; PSE and local e-wallets like Nequi are the standard payment rails.

How It Works

Coljuegos, Colombia's gambling regulatory authority, has licensed online operators since 2016 under Law 1753 of 2015. Licensed sites pay a percentage of GGR in taxes and must comply with local advertising and responsible-gambling rules.

Payment Methods

PSE (Pagos Seguros en Línea) is the required bank-transfer rail for licensed operators; Nequi and Efecty cover unbanked or cash-preferring users.

FAQ

Is online gambling legal in Colombia?

Yes, Colombia has a fully regulated online gambling market overseen by Coljuegos since 2016.

What is PSE?

PSE (Pagos Seguros en Línea) is Colombia's online banking payment gateway used for secure bank-to-merchant transfers, standard for licensed casino deposits.

Can unbanked players gamble online in Colombia?

Yes, via e-wallets like Nequi or cash networks like Efecty, which many licensed operators support.
